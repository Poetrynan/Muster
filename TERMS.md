## 3. Your account and your data

The application does not read, store or transmit your password. Your password
and any multi-factor verification are entered directly into your university's
own official login page and go directly to your university.

The application stores your signed-in session and any files you download on
your own computer only. There is no server operated by the developer, no
account to create, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting.

The only information that ever reaches the developer is a feedback message you
write and send yourself. If you use the feedback window inside the application,
your message is delivered to the developer's email through a third-party form
service, together with the application version, your interface language and
your system information. The application shows you exactly what will be sent
before you send it. Your email address is included only if you choose to type
it in. Nothing is ever sent in the background, and your courses, downloaded
files, login details and API key are never included.


## 4. Optional AI feature

If you choose to enable the AI summary feature, you must supply your own API
key for a third-party AI provider. When you do so, the text of the materials
you select is sent **from your computer directly to that provider**, under your
own account with them. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to
that transfer, and any charges are billed to you by them. The developer is not
a party to that arrangement and receives nothing.

This feature is off unless you turn it on.
